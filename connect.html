1

CONTROL ENCLOSURE

ESP32 · BQ24040 · TPS63020 · TPS55340

Holds the control board and the cell. Everything electrical lives in here except the actuator. Two connectors face outward: J1 for the battery, J2 for the solenoid.

2

PULL SOLENOID

TAU-S0837DL · 12 V · 1.2 A · 10 mm

Bare flying leads, no connector fitted from the factory. It only runs for the fraction of a second it takes to pull the plunger clear; the internal spring pushes it back.

3

LOCK BODY

spring detent, no electrical connection

Purely mechanical. The detent grabs the bolt on close and holds it there with no power at all, which is the whole point: a flat battery leaves the lock locked, not open.

Open items

Rail voltage. The solenoid wants 12 V. The boost stage is drawn at 5 V right now. One of those numbers has to move, and pulling 12 V out of a single cell is a different inductor and a different thermal story than 5 V.

Inrush. 1.2 A off an 18650 through a boost converter will sag the rail unless there is bulk capacitance sitting right at the actuator.

Cable exit. W2 has to get out of a box that is currently sealed. Needs a grommet or gland before the enclosure is final.

Antenna clearance. The lid is metal and sits directly over the patch. As drawn, the GPS will not see sky.
